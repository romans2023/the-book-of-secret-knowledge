# CSLB Law & Business study desk

A browser study guide for the California Contractors State License Board **Law and Business** exam: the seven-section outline, flashcards, and original practice questions.

This is a study aid. It is not a CSLB publication, not legal advice, and not a substitute for the [official Law and Business study guide (13E-LAW, March 2026)](https://www.cslb.ca.gov/Resources/StudyGuides/LawStudyGuide.pdf) or the California Contractors License Law & Reference Book. Confirm any rule you will use on a live job.

## Open it

From this folder:

```bash
python3 -m http.server 8765
```

Then open `http://127.0.0.1:8765`.

Progress (card scores, quiz scores, and the 14-day checkboxes) stays in this browser only.

## What is inside

- **Lessons** for the seven sections and the approximate weights in the March 2026 outline
- **Flashcards** for the dollar amounts, day counts, and distinctions the exam mixes up
- **The work**: section drills, a 20-question mix, a 40-question timed set, and a missed-question pile
- **Numbers** sheet and worked formulas (markup versus margin, overtime, liens, burden)
- **14-day plan** weighted toward contracts and employment

The trade exam is separate. A C-61 limited specialty has no trade exam; Law and Business is still required.
